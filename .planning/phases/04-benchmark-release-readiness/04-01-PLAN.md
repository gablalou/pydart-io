## Phase Goal (MVP — benchmark track)

**As a** prospective pydart adopter, **I want to** see a per-scenario benchmark of pydart vs pyarrow reporting both speed and peak memory, **so that** I can trust the "measurably faster than pyarrow" claim before I depend on it.

This plan delivers the thinnest complete vertical slice of that story: one scenario, measured end-to-end for both throughput and RSS.
